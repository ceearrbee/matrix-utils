Matrix Utils

A small, growing collection of client-side Matrix utilities. Use at your own discretion!

  • Matrix Mutuals

    Log into any Matrix homeserver and find the rooms you share with another user (MSC2666).

  • Room Re-Forge

    Upgrade a room to a new version and turn its encryption on or off by recreating it — with the tombstone, power levels, and member invites of a normal upgrade.
